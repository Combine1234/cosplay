## 3D runtime and mannequin

- Female and male body meshes: [Blender Human Base Meshes v1.4.1](https://www.blender.org/download/demo-files/), CC0. The demo applies an ivory retail-mannequin material and exports the meshes as GLB.
- Web renderer: [Three.js](https://threejs.org/), MIT License, vendored at version 0.180.0.
- Each product record in `studio-catalog.json` retains its source URL, creator, license, caveat, and 3D model path.
